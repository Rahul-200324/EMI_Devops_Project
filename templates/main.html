How EMI Is Calculated

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)ᴺ / ((1 + R)ᴺ - 1)
P: Principal loan amount
R: Monthly interest rate = Annual interest rate / 12 / 100
N: Total monthly installments = Loan tenure in years × 12
Total Interest: (EMI × N) − Principal
